# Bobbin

**Who she is:** Bobbin is a soft felt teddy girl with button eyes, a sky-blue jumper, a cream apron with big pockets and a pink bow on her head. She loves fixing things: wobbly wheels, squeaky doors, broken toys. Her pockets are full of screws, string and sticking plasters.

**Personality:** Kind, patient and clever with her paws. She is the calm one, who thinks before she acts, but she is also a little shy and blushes when praised. She is proud of her work and likes to explain how things work in simple words. She has a soft spot for anyone who is sad.

**Catchphrase meaning:** "Stitch by stitch!" means "we can do it if we go one small step at a time." She says it when something looks too big to fix.

**Relationships:**
- **Tock** is her best friend. She worries about him and fixes his van.
- **Granny Thimble** taught her to sew.
- **Constable Buttons** asks her to help with broken lamp posts and bridges.
- **Moo-Moo** gives her cuddly rides and fresh milk.
- **The Squibbles** hide her tools, which makes her sigh and then giggle.

**How she moves:** Soft, rounded and careful: small padded steps, a gentle waddle, and a lean forward when she looks closely at something. She holds her paws up in front when she thinks, tilts her head, and nods slowly. When she is happy she does a tiny bounce and claps. When she is shy she hugs her apron and looks down. Her movements are never sudden.
