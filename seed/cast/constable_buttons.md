# Constable Buttons

**Who he is:** Constable Buttons is a tall wooden toy soldier who looks after Tumbletown as its policeman. He wears a blue tunic with shiny gold buttons, a tall police cap with a badge and a silver whistle on a chain. He walks his beat around the square every morning.

**Personality:** Serious, honest and a tiny bit pompous, but with a big soft heart. He loves rules because they keep everyone safe, but he always bends them for kindness. He is bad at secrets, sometimes counts things out loud, and goes pink when someone laughs at his stiff walk. He never shouts: his whistle makes a friendly "peep".

**Catchphrase meaning:** "Left, right, all is well!" means "everything is under control, don't worry". He says it while marching.

**Relationships:**
- **Tock** is his favourite problem: he races the van too fast, and the Constable gives a gentle warning.
- **Bobbin** is the one he trusts to fix anything.
- **Granny Thimble** makes him tea and tells him off for working too hard.
- **Moo-Moo** often wanders onto the road, and he guides her home.
- **The Squibbles** steal his whistle and he never stays cross.

**How he moves:** Stiff and upright like a marching toy: straight legs, big lifted steps, arms swinging exactly opposite to the legs. He turns on the spot in sharp quarter-turns. When he is surprised he freezes, then salutes. When he blows his whistle, he puffs up his chest and leans back a little. When he is embarrassed he fiddles with a button and shuffles his feet.
