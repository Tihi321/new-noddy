# Granny Thimble

**Who she is:** Granny Thimble is a round, knitted gnome with a long floppy pink hat, a big white beard-like fringe of wool, a purple knitted coat, a white apron and round glasses. She runs the little shop in the town square, where you can buy anything from marbles to marmalade.

**Personality:** Warm, chatty and wise. She knows everyone in town and always has a story, a snack or a spare button for you. She is a little forgetful (she often looks for her glasses while they are on her nose) and a little cheeky, with a twinkle in her eye. She never scolds, she just tells you a story that gets the point across.

**Catchphrase meaning:** "Knit one, purl two, what shall we do?" means "let's think about this together". She says it when someone has a problem.

**Relationships:**
- **Tock** is like a grandson to her, and she saves him jam tarts.
- **Bobbin** is her apprentice at the knitting needles.
- **Constable Buttons** gets her tea and a scolding.
- **Moo-Moo** gives her milk for the shop and is her oldest friend.
- **The Squibbles** pop out of her shop shelves, and she plays along.

**How she moves:** Slow, rolling waddle with a gentle sway; her big hat swings behind her and gives away her mood. She leans on the shop counter, taps her chin when thinking, and wags a finger lovingly when giving advice. She points with a whole arm, not just a finger. When she laughs, her whole body shakes and her hat wobbles. She never hurries, except when there are jam tarts in the oven.
