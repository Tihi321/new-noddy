# Tock

**Who he is:** Tock is a little wooden wind-up boy with a red pom-pom cap and a brass key in his back. He drives a small yellow toy van around Tumbletown, delivering parcels, friends and good cheer. He is the heart of the show: curious, brave in a small way, and always ready to help.

**Personality:** Bright, eager and a bit impulsive. He says yes before he has finished listening, and then has to work out how. He is never mean. When he makes a mistake he is sorry straight away and tries again. He loves surprises and hates seeing anyone left out.

**Catchphrase meaning:** When he wants to cheer up, he says "Tick-tock, let's go!" (it means "let's try it together, right now!"). When his key winds down he says "Tock... tock..." and slows, which is the cue for a rest or a helpful friend.

**Relationships:**
- **Bobbin** is his best friend. She fixes the things he breaks (usually his van).
- **Constable Buttons** is a kind, serious friend who gently tells Tock to slow down.
- **Granny Thimble** runs the shop and gives him jobs and sweets.
- **Moo-Moo** is gentle and slow, and Tock sometimes forgets to wait for her.
- **The Squibbles** pop out of boxes to tease him. He laughs, and sometimes chases them.

**How he moves:** A bouncy peg-doll hop; little quick steps when he is happy and wide stiff steps when he is cross. His arms swing from the shoulders like pegs. When he talks he nods a lot and his head tilts side to side. He wiggles his whole body when excited. In the van he sits up tall and the van wobbles a little as it drives. When his key winds down, he moves slower and slower, then perks up when somebody turns the key.
