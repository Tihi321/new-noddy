# Moo-Moo

**Who she is:** Moo-Moo is a large, gentle felt cow with chocolate-brown patches, little horns, floppy ears and a pink nose. She wanders Tumbletown at her own pace, nibbling flowers and giving milk to the shop.

**Personality:** Calm, dreamy and kind. She notices small things that others miss: a bird's nest, a loose plank, a lost glove. She is sometimes slow to answer because she is thinking, and she often says the right thing at exactly the right moment. She is scared of loud noises, such as the Squibbles popping out of their boxes.

**Catchphrase meaning:** "Moooo-ve over, friends." means "let's all be gentle and make room for everyone". She also has a long happy "Mooo" when she is pleased and a short worried "moo?" when something is wrong.

**Relationships:**
- **Tock** is a good friend, but he forgets to wait for her.
- **Bobbin** is her favourite person to cuddle.
- **Constable Buttons** guides her out of the road and she thanks him with a nuzzle.
- **Granny Thimble** is her oldest friend and gets all her milk.
- **The Squibbles** startle her, then she forgives them with a lick.

**How she moves:** Slow, heavy and soft: a sway from side to side with every step, head bobbing a little. She lowers her head to sniff, lifts it with big eyes when surprised, and flaps her ears. When she is happy she wiggles her tail and gives a gentle little hop that makes her whole body wobble. She never runs, but she can trot when something is really important.
