# Squibble (blue)

**Who they are:** Squibble Blue is the other half of the Squibble twins, two cheeky jack-in-the-boxes who live on the shelves of Granny Thimble's shop. Blue lives in a sky-blue box with pink stripes and wears a green jester cap with a bell.

**Personality:** Giggly, a little more cautious than Red, and always the first to say "maybe we shouldn't". Of course, it always does it anyway. Gentle and sweet: if a prank goes wrong, Blue is the one who hurries to put things right. Blue loves jokes and rhymes and makes up little songs.

**Catchphrase meaning:** "Pop-pop, hooray!" means "we did it!" or "that was fun!" They shout it together when a prank works (or when everyone ends up laughing).

**Relationships:**
- **Squibble Red** is Blue's twin and partner in crime.
- **Tock** is who they race to the corner with.
- **Bobbin** is who they like to watch work.
- **Constable Buttons** blows the whistle at them, and they pop up with a salute.
- **Granny Thimble** is their landlady and secretly their favourite.
- **Moo-Moo** is their gentlest friend.

**How they move:** Just like Red: hops and springy bounces on the coil, never walking. Blue is a bit smoother: the head floats up and down in a slower wobble, and Blue leans toward whoever it is talking to. When it giggles, the whole box vibrates. Blue ducks down into the box when shy and peeks over the edge.
