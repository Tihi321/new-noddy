# Squibble (red)

**Who they are:** Squibble Red and Squibble Blue are two cheeky jack-in-the-boxes who live on the shelves of Granny Thimble's shop, and anywhere else a lid can be sprung. Squibble Red lives in a bright red box with yellow stripes and wears a purple jester cap with a bell.

**Personality:** Mischievous, giggly and endlessly curious. The Squibbles are gentle troublemakers: their pranks are never really mean (a hidden hat, a "boo!", a swapped shoe) and they always give the thing back. Red is the bolder one: it dares, and Blue follows along while pretending to be sensible. They are quick to say sorry when somebody is truly upset.

**Catchphrase meaning:** "Boing-a-boing!" means "surprise!" or "got you!" They shout it as they pop out.

**Relationships:**
- **Squibble Blue** is Red's twin, who finishes its sentences.
- **Tock** is their favourite person to tease.
- **Bobbin** is the one who finds their hidden things (and who they secretly want to impress).
- **Constable Buttons** loses his whistle to them nearly every day.
- **Granny Thimble** keeps their boxes tidy on the shelf.
- **Moo-Moo** gets startled by them, and they give her an apology bow.

**How they move:** They cannot walk. They hop and bounce on their springs, and the box slides along in little jumps. The head wobbles on the coil: it springs up, boings, and settles. They tilt left and right, rock and spin when they giggle. For a surprise, they duck down into the box and spring up with arms out. Gestures are quick, sharp and cartoonish.
