Toyland Wood is the default look. It has chunky painted wooden peg dolls, felt and knit cloth, a miniature tabletop town with a painted cardboard sky, a warm soft key light with a cool fill, shallow miniature depth of field, and a touch of film grain. Animation is shot "on twos" (12 unique poses per second, shown at 24) with a tiny hand-placement wobble on every pose, like real stop-motion.
